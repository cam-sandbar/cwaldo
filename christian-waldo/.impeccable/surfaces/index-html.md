Scope: the single-page homepage at `index.html`, shipped as one self-contained file. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: artists and bands looking for a producer, artists and labels sending sessions out to mix and master, and supervisors/agencies commissioning music. Job: decide in under a minute whether to hand this person a project. Action: one inquiry path that names the work. Proof: listening — no credits, clients, numbers or prices exist yet, so every portfolio entry and the about copy ship as authored placeholders, labeled for replacement, and nothing commercial is invented.

Constraints: static HTML/CSS, one file, no build step. Palette pinned by the user to light blue, white and grey.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A producer's catalogue is a shelf of tape boxes, so the page is the vault you walk rather than a headline with a waveform under it. It refuses the category arrangement — portrait hero, three service cards, contact form — and the near-black-plus-neon page that is its predictable opposite.

OWN-WORLD: Box-board grey (#8d9aa4 / #c4ccd2) as the ground, gummed-label white (#f7f9fa), ink near-black (#2d363d), and one reserved chinagraph blue (#2f6fb0). Materials: grey board, typed gummed labels bordered in ink, shelf steel, reel hubs drawn as concentric rings. Typed labels set in Courier Prime; the index card, spines and actions set in Archivo Narrow caps with wide tracking. Hard edges, hairline-to-3px rules, no soft shadow doing a line's work. Recognisable with every word removed: a steel rail, a run of bordered boxes, one box forward in blue, one empty slot.

STORY: The visitor understands that one person kept the whole record — written, tracked, mixed, mastered — and that the work is here to be heard, not described. They believe it because the shelf is specific and physical rather than claimed. They put a project in the empty slot at the end of the run.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The vault index card is flush to the top-left corner, white on the board ground, carrying the name at display scale over a rule and the four services; the positioning line and the now-playing cue sit to its right across the upper band. Below, a steel rail runs edge to edge and a run of six reel boxes hangs from it, full-bleed left and right, each box a bordered card with a typed label, a drawn reel hub and a spine line. One box stands pulled forward in chinagraph blue, playing. The seventh slot is empty — a dashed outline with a blue blank label reading START A PROJECT, and that is the primary action, unmistakable at the end of the run.

FORM: The Tape Vault — studio tape libraries, reel boxes, typed spine labels, shelf index cards. Candidate 4 of seven on the grounded list (session take sheet, sleeve back, test pressing, tape vault, studio room, riso bill, console); assigned by the roll. Raised by four declined challengers: three materials only with controls that take no colour, punched or scored instead (depot blind); no midtone, every edge lit or unlit (Weimar bill); one reserved brightest value spent only on what is under attention (cloud edge); attention as the state model, the box you are on comes forward and fades back with afterglow (understory). seed: 38b5a5c4f46e4d96

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
