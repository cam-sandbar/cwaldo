# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS — one self-contained `.html` file, no build step, no external framework (user requirement).

## Users

Independent artists and bands looking for a producer to make a record with; artists and labels sending finished sessions out to be mixed and mastered; and music supervisors, agencies and filmmakers commissioning or licensing music. They arrive from a referral, a credit, or a DM, usually on a phone, deciding within a minute whether this is someone they can hand a project to.

## Product Purpose

The homepage for Christian Waldo, a music producer. It exists to make a prospective client confident enough to start a conversation about a specific project. Success is an inquiry that names the work.

## Positioning

One producer across the whole chain — production, mixing, mastering, and commissioned/custom music — rather than a single-service shop. Working with Christian means one set of ears from first idea to delivered master.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients evaluate producers by listening and by credits, often while doing something else. Projects begin as an email or DM describing a song, a release date, and a budget. Scheduling, stems, and reference tracks follow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services offered: production, mixing, mastering, commissioned/custom music. (Undecided: whether these are presented as named packages or as one open offering — the page presents them as capabilities, not priced packages.)
- Deliverable is one HTML file; portfolio and about content ship as placeholders for the user to replace.
- No pricing, no client names, no testimonials, no press, no streaming numbers: none have been confirmed and none may be invented as real.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Christian Waldo.
- Palette pinned by the user: light blue, white, grey.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. No real track titles, artwork, credits, client list, photo, or bio exist. Every portfolio entry and the about copy are authored placeholders, labeled as such for the user to swap in real material.

## Product Principles

1. Listening is the proof; everything else is framing.
2. One inquiry path, always within reach.
3. One pair of ears across the chain — say it as a fact about the work, not as a slogan.
4. Never state a credit, client, number, or price that has not been confirmed.
5. Phone-first: the decision usually happens on a small screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement established; standard WCAG AA contrast and keyboard operability apply.
